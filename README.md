# Calli

A quiet, local-first Mac app for freewriting, drafting and publishing, part of [Prometheus Labs](https://github.com/promethevs-labs).

## Why "Calli"

Calli is short for Calliope, the eldest of the nine Muses in Greek myth. She was the muse of eloquence and epic poetry, usually shown holding a writing tablet, and Hesiod called her the chief of the Muses. Prometheus Labs names its projects after Greek myth (Argus Index, Nous Codex, Hermes Studio), and Calliope fits an app whose whole job is helping you write.

## Status

Calli is a fork of [freewrite](https://github.com/farzaa/freewrite) by Farza Majeed. Right now it is the upstream app unchanged. The plan is to grow it into one control panel for writing, drafting and sending work out to Notion and Substack.

## Build

- Clone the repo.
- Open `freewrite.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
- Click build.
